Privacy Policy

Block Puzzle: Brain Games (the "app"), by Umamaheswar. Last updated: 7 November 2026.

What the app stores on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, settings, saved games) is stored only on your device. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or microphone.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads). AdMob may use the Android advertising ID and similar device information to show and measure ads, and to limit ad frequency. Ads are limited to family-friendly content. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent, and you can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy Options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses data.

Buying "Remove Ads" turns the ads off.

Leaderboard

If the online leaderboard is turned on, the app signs you in anonymously (Firebase Authentication) and stores a random nickname generated by the app, your total stars, levels and Block Mode best score in Google Cloud Firestore, so that rankings can be shown. No real name or contact detail is collected. The nickname is chosen by the app, not typed by you.

Purchases

In-app purchases (Remove Ads, coin packs) are handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It does not contain chat or user-generated content.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the Google services named above (AdMob, Play Billing, Firebase) to run the app. Leaderboard entries can be deleted on request: contact us with your nickname.

Your choices

Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device. Use Settings > Reset Progress to erase game data in the app.
